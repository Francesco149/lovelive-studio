# Recipe book

What works on this PC for each kind of picture, kept current by the agent after every judged round
(CLAUDE.md section 4). One section per kind of picture; each with a full example prompt, the body drawing and
strength, the seed of the best result, and the user's words. Replace stale advice instead of stacking it.

- Example: the Studio's "Idol on stage". Kit examples and why they work: docs/guide/index.html.
